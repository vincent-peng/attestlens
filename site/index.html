How it works

Scan → Map → Decide → Gate. Deterministic every time.

1

Scan

Scanner adapters collect evidence from codebases, URLs, supply chains, and infrastructure. No network needed at runtime.

2

Map

Findings map to jurisdiction obligations — AU APP/NDB, US CCPA, EU GDPR, SG PDPA. Same evidence, different citations.

3

Opinion

Structured deploy decision with evidence. Agents act on it directly. Humans review uncertain cases. CI gates on it.

4

Gate

Exit codes for CI. SARIF for GitHub. MCP for agents. Block the deploy or let it through — deterministic, not probabilistic.
